# Educational Methodology & Pedagogical Principles

**Project:** QubitPath — Adaptive Visual Quantum Learning Lab  
**Challenge:** WISER Summer Program 2026 Industry Challenge Submission

---

## 1. Pedagogical Foundations

QubitPath applies ten core evidence-based educational principles to scaffold quantum computing for beginners:

1. **Scaffolded Learning:** Begins with single-qubit states $|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$, progresses to unitary single-qubit gates, measurement collapse, and concludes with two-qubit Bell state entanglement.
2. **Prediction Before Observation:** Prompts learners to predict state or probability outcomes before applying gates or executing measurement shots.
3. **Immediate Formative Feedback:** Formats incorrect answer responses into misconception-specific explanations, calling out physical principles and suggesting targeted review modules.
4. **Multiple Representations:** Presents quantum states simultaneously across four representations:
   - Plain-language descriptions
   - Dirac mathematical notation ($|\psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle$)
   - 3D/2D Bloch sphere vector projections
   - Probability amplitude bars & sampling histograms
5. **Active Learning:** Replaces passive reading with interactive state vector manipulation, gate application, and seedable shot sampling.
6. **Retrieval Practice:** Incorporates formative concept checks in every module alongside pre- and post-assessments.
7. **Misconception Correction:** Tracks eight documented beginner misconceptions to provide tailored instructional remedies.
8. **Low-Stakes Assessment:** Pre- and post-assessments allow retries and clear explanations without punitive barriers.
9. **Visible Learning Progress:** Quantifies pre- vs post-test score gain, percentage-point improvement, and misconception reduction.
10. **Accessibility & Inclusivity:** Implements high-contrast dark navy UI, ARIA keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion support.
